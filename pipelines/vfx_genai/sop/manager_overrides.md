# Manager's overrides (from the VFX repo's CLAUDE.md)

These override the SOPs wherever they conflict.

- **At most 3 people per company** on a tab, counting existing rows.
- **A title that comes only from a database is acceptable but marked `Medium`.** Say so when presenting rows. SOP 02 section 15 is stricter, so prefer people confirmed on the company site.
- **Always exclude India:** company, team and person. This overrides SOP 01 section 6.2, which lists India.
- **Reject large agency networks and brand groups** (Dentsu, Havas, Publicis, WPP, Omnicom...), broadcasters, marketing, SEO, PR, web and branding agencies, and tool vendors.
- **Quality beats volume.** Never lower the quality bar to hit a number.
